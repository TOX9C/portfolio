About

The operator

The overlap between AI, security, and full-stack is where I live.

I'm a third-year Engineering student studying Cybersecurity and Cloud Computing at NTU in Iraq. What drives me is building things that think, move fast, and actually work.

I've trained machine learning models to predict football match outcomes at 58.77% accuracy (beating bookmaker baselines), built a ransomware detector from scratch, and shipped a multi-agent IDE written in Rust. I also worked part-time in IT at TAQA, one of the largest companies in Iraq.

Outside of code, I run a home server with Jellyfin, Pi-hole, and Tailscale. Why rent infrastructure when you can build your own?
